### Q1
Which device connects a home network to the internet service provider's line?
- A) A modem
- B) A print server
- C) A sound card
- D) A KVM switch

**Answer: A**
A modem terminates the provider's line and hands connectivity to the local network. The other devices serve unrelated roles: printing, audio, and peripheral switching.

### Q2
A user complains that typed characters appear on screen only when the keyboard cable is reseated. What is the most likely component at fault?
- A) The video cable
- B) The monitor stand
- C) The speaker jack
- D) The keyboard connection

**Answer: D**
Symptoms that change when a connector is reseated point at that connector. Video, enclosure hardware, and audio paths do not carry keystrokes.

### Q3
Which battery technology would you expect inside a modern laptop's removable power pack?
- A) Nickel-cadmium
- B) Lead-acid
- C) Lithium-ion
- D) Zinc-carbon

**Answer: C**
Lithium-ion packs dominate modern laptops for their energy density and weight. Nickel-cadmium and lead-acid are older or heavier chemistries; zinc-carbon is a dry-cell chemistry.

### Q4
What is the first step before replacing a laptop's internal drive?
- A) Update the drive's marketing literature
- B) Power off and disconnect the power source
- C) Delete the recovery partition
- D) Reinstall the display panel

**Answer: B**
Powering off and disconnecting power is the safety prerequisite for internal component work. The other actions either come after the swap or are destructive mistakes.
